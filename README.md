# ⚖️ CaseLens AI

CaseLens AI is an AI-powered legal research and case analysis platform designed to assist lawyers, law firms, law students, and legal researchers.

> 🚧 **Project Status:** Currently under active development.

---

## ✨ Vision

CaseLens AI aims to simplify legal research by helping users:

- Upload legal case documents
- Generate AI-assisted case summaries
- Identify possible evidence gaps
- Detect contradictions
- Discover similar precedents
- Build legal arguments
- Generate structured case reports

> **Disclaimer:** CaseLens AI provides AI-assisted legal research and does **not** constitute legal advice.
